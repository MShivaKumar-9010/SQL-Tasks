Question 1
Display the customer name, city, product, quantity, total amount (quantity × price) for customers who:
* belong to Hyderabad or Chennai
* purchased products between ₹2,000 and ₹70,000
* payment mode is UPI or Card
* order by total amount descending
* display only top 5 records.
 select c.cust_name,c.city,o.product,o.qnty,(o.qnty*o.product)as total_amount from customer as c left join orders as o on c.cust_id=o.cust_id where (c.city='hyd' or c.city='chennai') and(price between 2000 and 70000) and (o.pymt_mode='UPI' or o.pymt_mode='card') order by total_amount desc limit 5;


Question 2
Display each category along with
* total sales
* average sales
* maximum sale
* minimum sale
* number of orders
Only include categories
* having total sales greater than ₹50,000
* average sales greater than ₹10,000
Sort by highest sales.
 Select catgry,sum(qnty*price),avg(qnty*price),max(qnty*price),min(qnty*price),count(ord_id) from customer inner join orders on customer.cust_id = orders.cust_id group by catgry having sum(qnty*price) > 50000 and avg(qnty*price) > 10000 order by sum(qnty*price) desc;


Question 3
Find customers who have purchased more than one different product.
Display
* customer name
* city
* number of products purchased
* total quantity ordered
Sort by total quantity descending.
select cust_name,city,count(distinct product),sum(qnty) from customer as c inner join orders as o on c.cust_id = o.cust_id group by cust_name,city having count(distinct product)>1 order by sum(qnty) desc;


Question 4
Display the details of customers who purchased products whose total amount is greater than the average total amount of all orders.
Display
* customer name
* product
* quantity
* price
* total amount
Sort by total amount descending.
 select c.cust_name,o.product,o.qnty,o.price,(qnty * price) as total_amount from customer as c inner join orders as o on c.cust_id = o.cust_id where (qnty*price) > ( select avg(qnty*price) from orders) order by total_amount desc;


Question 5
Display customers who
* purchased Electronics
* age greater than 24
* city starts with 'H' or ends with 'i'
* payment mode is not Cash
Display
* customer name
* city
* product
* payment mode
* total amount
Sort alphabetically by customer name.
 select c.cust_name,c.city,o.product,o.pymt_mode,(qnty*price) as total_amount from customer as c right join orders as o on c.cust_id = o.cust_id where (catgry ='Electronics') and (age>24) and (city like"H%" or city like"%i") and (pymt_mode='UPI' or pymt_mode='Card') order by cust_name asc;


Question 6
Generate a report showing
* customer name
Only display customers whose total spending is greater than ₹30,000.
Sort by spending descending.
select cust_name,(qnty*price) as total_spendings from customer as c left join orders as o on c.cust_id=o.cust_id where (qnty*price) > 30000 order by (qnty*price) desc;


Question 7
Find the top 3 customers who spent the highest amount.
Display
* customer name
* city
* total orders
* total quantity
* total spending
Only include customers
* having at least 2 orders.
 select cust_name,city,sum(ord_id),sum(qnty) as total_qnty,sum(qnty*price) as total_spent from customer as c right join orders as o on c.cust_id=o .cust_id group by cust_name,city having sum(qnty) >= 2;


Question 8
Display products that have been ordered by customers
* from Hyderabad or Bangalore
* age less than 30
* quantity greater than or equal to 2
Display
* product
* category
* customer name
* city
* total amount
Sort by category and then total amount descending.
 select product,catgry,cust_name,city,(qnty*price) as total_amount from customer as c inner join orders as o on c.cust_id = o.cust_id where (city = 'hyd' or city = 'banglore') and (age<30) and (qnty >=2) order by catgry,(qnty*price) desc;


Question 9
Display every customer along with
* customer name
* city
* total orders
If a customer has not placed any order, still display the customer with total orders as 0.
Sort by total orders descending.
 select cust_name,city,count(ord_id) from customer as c left join orders as o on c.cust_id = o. cust_id group by cust_name,city order by count(ord_id) desc;


Question 10 (Very Hard)
Prepare a sales report showing
* customer name
* city
* category
* total quantity
* total sales
* average order value
Only include customers
* whose age is between 25 and 40
* payment mode is Card or UPI
* category is Electronics or Furniture
Exclude
* orders where quantity = 1
Display only categories
* having total sales greater than ₹50,000
wSort by
1. Category
2. Total Sales DESC
3. Customer Name ASC
Display only the first 5 rows.
select cust_name,city,catgry,sum(qnty),avg(ord_id),sum(qnty*price) as total_sales from customer as c inner join orders as o on c.cust_id= o.cust_id where (age>=25 and age<=40) and (pymt_mode='card' or pymt_mode='UPI') and (catgry='Electronics' or catgry='Furniture') and (qnty <> 1) group by cust_name,city,catgry having sum(qnty*price) > 50000 order by catgry,sum(qnty*price) desc,cust_name asc limit 5;
