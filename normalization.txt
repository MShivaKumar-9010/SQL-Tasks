2. Write an SQL query that lists all orders with the customer's name, product name, quantity, and total price per line item, demonstrating that the data can be retrieved without duplication.
select
 o.order_number,
 c.customer_name,
 p.product_name,
 oi.quantity
(oi.quantity*oi.unit_price) as total_price
  from orders o
  join customer c on o.customer_id=c.customer_id
  join orderitem oi on o.order_id=oi.order_id
  join product p on oi.product_id=p.product_id;
